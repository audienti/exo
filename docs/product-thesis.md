# Product Thesis

## One line

Exo is the control room for agentic GTM.

## What changed

The old outbound software frame assumed the product itself had to do the work:

- find prospects
- research accounts
- write messages
- automate follow-up
- own the infrastructure

That frame is broken.

Google, Anthropic, and OpenAI are all absorbing the generic agent layer:

- background agents
- memory
- browser and tool use
- recurring work
- multi-agent supervision
- artifact generation

That means research, drafting, prompting, and generic automation are becoming commodity capabilities.

## New frame

The product is not the worker.

The product is the system that lets an operator direct workers.

Exo should sit between increasingly capable agents and real customer-facing business action.

## What Exo owns

High confidence:

- canonical state
- evidence
- approvals
- policy
- execution history
- CRM writeback
- artifact-shaped review surfaces

Exo should be the place where the operator can:

- see what changed
- see what matters
- inspect proposed actions
- approve, edit, reject, or escalate
- handle exceptions
- preserve mission context across accounts and contacts

## What Exo is not building

Do not build:

- a proprietary writer meant to catch up to frontier models
- a generic managed-agent cloud
- a giant workflow builder
- a broad multichannel outbound suite
- a CRM clone
- a "replace your SDR team" product

## Product promise

Exo makes one great GTM operator work like a coordinated team of humans and agents.
