# Competitor Map

## The wrong framing

If Exo is framed as an AI SDR, it gets trapped in a crowded category and loses.

The correct comparison is not just "who automates outbound."

The better question is:

- who is trying to own the signal-to-action control layer for agentic GTM?

## Competitor classes

### 1. Closest conceptual competitors

#### Common Room

Position:

- buyer intelligence
- signals
- AI agents
- actioning from unified profiles

Why it matters:

- closest to the "signal plus context plus action" motion

Risk:

- could own the intelligence layer before Exo exists

#### Unify

Position:

- signal-based outbound
- AI agents
- plays
- approval gates

Why it matters:

- closest to the "signal fires, play runs, operator supervises" model

Risk:

- strong direct overlap on signal-driven execution

#### Nooks

Position:

- research
- sequencing
- dialing
- coaching
- workspace for outbound teams

Why it matters:

- closer to the operator workspace and command-center side

Risk:

- could pull Exo toward being a rep workspace instead of an operator control room

### 2. CRM-native incumbents

#### Salesforce Agentforce

Position:

- prospecting
- research
- next best action
- quoting
- native workflow embedding

Risk:

- strongest incumbent if the product collapses into a CRM feature set

#### HubSpot Breeze

Position:

- prospecting agent inside HubSpot
- outcome-oriented pricing
- native CRM context

Risk:

- dangerous for SMB and midmarket if Exo looks like a thin HubSpot add-on

### 3. Revenue platform and data-stack players

#### Apollo

Position:

- data
- enrichment
- outbound execution
- AI assistant
- MCP path into frontier-model workflows

Risk:

- strongest "DIY but connected" competitor

#### Clay

Position:

- workflow and enrichment substrate

Risk:

- attracts the power-user audience if Exo fails to beat spreadsheet-plus-workflow bricolage

### 4. Autonomous AI SDR vendors

#### Artisan
#### 11x
#### Regie

Position:

- replace or automate the SDR role
- end-to-end prospecting and outreach

Why they matter:

- they compete for budget and attention

Why they are not the right category:

- Exo should not promise full autonomy
- Exo should promise operator amplification and governed action

### 5. Real monster competitor: DIY

This is the actual benchmark:

- Claude / Codex / Gemini
- Sales Navigator
- HubSpot
- Apollo / Clay
- spreadsheets
- browser automation
- scripts
- notes

If Exo is not dramatically better than this stack for a power user, it has no reason to exist.

## Positioning against the field

Against AI SDR vendors:

- we amplify the operator, we do not pretend to replace the operator

Against CRM-native suites:

- we are the control room above fragmented systems, not just a feature inside one CRM

Against signal vendors:

- signals alone are not enough; Exo turns them into governed action

Against DIY stacks:

- stop rebuilding the same brittle state machine in prompts, spreadsheets, and automations
