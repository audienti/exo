# Customer Discovery

## Who Exo is for

Do not target "sales teams" in the abstract.

Exo is for operators who already use AI and already feel the pain of running GTM work across too many tools.

## Best-fit early users

### 1. Founder-led outbound operators

- B2B founders still personally involved in pipeline generation
- already using Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, or Gemini manually
- close enough to the work to feel state loss and review pain

### 2. Agency and consultancy GTM operators

- owner
- delivery lead
- head of outbound
- anyone running motions for multiple clients with a messy stack

### 3. RevOps and GTM systems power users

- people stitching together Clay, Apollo, HubSpot, spreadsheets, enrichment, alerts, and browser tasks
- already experimenting with semi-agentic workflows

### 4. Signal-driven outbound leaders

- small-to-mid teams
- high-value outbound
- care about timing, context, and review more than brute-force volume

## People to avoid early

- big-company VP Sales who does not touch tools
- procurement-style buyers
- CRM admins with no outbound pain
- people shopping for a full SDR replacement fantasy
- large enterprises whose first questions are permissions, territories, and governance committees

Those conversations will drag the product back into the wrong frame.

## Discovery goal

Do not ask:

- "Would you buy an AI sales tool?"

Ask:

- "Would you run your daily signal-to-action work inside Exo instead of across Claude, browser tabs, CRM, spreadsheets, and notes?"

## Discovery phases

### Phase 1: problem validation

Ask:

- how do you run this today?
- where does state get lost?
- what do you still have to supervise manually?
- what do you not trust agents to do alone?
- where does the process break?
- what is spread across too many tools?

### Phase 2: workflow playback

Walk through:

- signal arrives
- account brief updates
- action is proposed
- operator reviews or edits
- reply arrives
- CRM commit happens

### Phase 3: design partner conversion

The best design partner says:

- "I already have this exact problem."
- "I can give you a real account set."
- "I will use this every week if it works."

## Initial meeting mix

Start with 12 conversations:

- 3 founder-led outbound operators
- 3 agency or consultancy outbound leads
- 3 RevOps or GTM systems power users
- 3 warm design-partner style contacts from the current network
